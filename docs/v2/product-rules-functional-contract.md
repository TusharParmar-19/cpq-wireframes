# Phase 1 — Product Catalog & Rules
Existing MA-006A owns categories, product groups, exact models, manufacturers, parts, product templates, documents and catalog commercial inputs. Do not duplicate those masters. Control page adds versioned model-pair selection rules: A requires B, or A cannot coexist with B. Model IDs are stable; two distinct existing active models are required to enable a rule. Draft/Active/Inactive, warning/block, employee guidance, and previous versions. No active rules seeded. Equivalent active duplicates rejected. Existing fixed sample options (630A floor mounting) remain unchanged.
Active checks apply to configuration handoff and editable quotation review. Drafts may be saved with a failed pair check; warning is informational, block prevents handoff or review. Direct orders bypass new pair checks since equipment purchases are not necessarily an engineered assembly. Review captures the current active rule set before fingerprinting; locked older quotations lacking a snapshot are not retrospectively rechecked against new rules. New quote revisions and duplicates re-evaluate current active rules. Orders, prices, quote totals and customer history are unaffected. Rule changes do not mutate prior captured definitions. Current configuration drafts are rechecked on handoff.
Browser-only storage; no authentication, engineering certification, cable/string/MPPT calculations, automated BOM or rule administration permissions enforced. Master Admin defaults list the control permission for future backend work; existing custom role assignments are not changed. Formal engineering and advanced approval policies remain separate.

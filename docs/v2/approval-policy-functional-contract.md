# Approval policies and revisions (browser demo)
Global/company/branch policy versions, branch > company > global; explicit saved policy required. Technical and commercial stages mandatory; escalation based on combined discount, minimum margin, invoice total, optional manual-rate, GST assumption and contribution flags. Roles require approvals page + view + decide, active scoped employee, no self-review or repeated reviewer. Policy and candidates captured at submission; eligibility rechecked on decision. No authenticated permission enforcement.
Submitted/returned/rejected/reviewed/dispatched revisions locked. New revision preserves same quotation ID, full copied snapshot, captured identity and amounts, clears current approval and dispatch. Comparison shows commercial values, BOQ changes and terms. Accepted quotes excluded until controlled amendment/order design. Historical seed metadata is not a full snapshot. Legacy demo reviews without captured policy require new revision before dispatch.
Browser storage only, not multi-user safe or immutable backend. Saved role grants are not silently elevated; MA-003 must be configured. All defaults are demo assumptions. No real approval or issue.

# Phase 1 — received Vendor Quotations (V2)
Business scope: Master SRS 11.13 (vendor quotation handling), PG-070–072, AC-P1-026/027. This extends Vendor Master as a second tab; do not modify V1.

- Separate internal ID, vendor reference, entity, optional branch and enquiry; received date, source date and validity. Draft and recorded offer are distinct. Each offer can hold multiple exact catalog SKU/service lines or custom descriptive lines; the latter are record-only until mapped. Unit, quantity, purchase rate before GST (INR), tax percentage, additional freight/charges, optional charge tax, delivery/payment terms and remarks. Totals are illustrative; unknown taxes are flagged.
- Capture filename/size/type metadata for original supplier evidence. Document attached is independent of verification. Review includes explicit decision and remark, simulated reviewer/date; no real file or authenticated approval. A verified offer is locked; copy as new with distinct reference for a changed offer; archive retains history. Expired/archived offers not available for fresh application.
- Use in Pricing & Costing: explicit per-line selection, exact stable SKU/service ID, unit and quantity, company and optional branch/enquiry, INR, active supplier and valid verified demo source. Copy pre-tax unit purchase rate and source snapshot into the draft's internal cost only; no selling-rate/catalog mutation, no automatic freight/tax duplication. Quantity or scope change requires clearing/reselecting; review checks current validity. Previous issued/sent sample quotations are not mutated.
- Policy choices for demo: exact quoted quantity (no automatic volume-tier assumptions); before-tax purchase rate; separate tax/freight; valid verified metadata only; custom items not auto-matched. Real Finance/Purchase policy, role-separated verification, document storage, multi-currency/tax, rate exception policy, server-side validation and audit remain Laravel work. Advanced RFQ creation/sending and vendor comparison remain Phase 2.

Test: add vendor offer; draft save/reopen; add product and service; record with evidence metadata; verify separately; check expired/missing-evidence and duplicate cases; use rate in pricing; verify internal cost not sale rate and customer preview excludes source; edit quantity/scope; copy/archiving preserves history; test quick create; browser refresh.

Pricing shortcut: Record supplier offer opens the Vendor Quotations tab in a new browser tab with fictional company/branch/enquiry and exact item/quantity preselected where valid; it never sends rates or customer personal information in the URL. After recording and verifying the offer, return to the still-open customer draft and Refresh rates. Applying remains explicit. This workflow does not require opening a separate sidebar module.

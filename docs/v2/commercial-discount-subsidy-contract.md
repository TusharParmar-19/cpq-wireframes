# V2 advanced commercial terms (browser-only)

- New quotations support item and overall percentage or fixed discounts. A fixed item discount applies to the entire line. Overall discount applies after item discounts, including separate customer charges. Integer-paise allocation reconciles all rows. GST is calculated on discounted amounts. Included sub-services and private costs are excluded.
- Existing quotation calculations stay unchanged unless an editable item-GST draft explicitly opts in. Its existing percentage discount is retained. All material edits reset demo review. Captured tax and rates are not refreshed from masters.
- Estimated subsidy: full invoice remains payable; projected customer cost is informational. Confirmed third-party contribution: simulated split between customer share and third-party receivable. Neither changes invoice revenue, GST, or margin; require scheme and confirmation reference for review. Actual eligibility, tax treatment, claims and collection are not implemented.
- Nearest rupee rounding is optional, default no rounding. Seven arithmetic checks verify discount allocation, net, GST bases/split, invoice and contribution. Invalid excessive discounts/subsidies block save; missing tax or subsidy confirmation blocks review but not draft saving.
- Cost-plus application is blocked while item or fixed overall discounts are active; apply target first, then negotiate and review actual margin. No production approval or PDF issued.
- Customer preview shows item and overall discounts, GST and funding assumptions; no internal costs, margin or private references.

# Phase 1: Reports and activity logs
Read-only browser demonstration. Reuses master and saved data, current quotation revision only, and captured order calculation snapshots. No real invoicing, cash, authenticated actor, permission enforcement, or append-only audit. Company/branch filters scope report cards and rows; status/module and search scope selected table. Unique customers match any linked selling scope and are counted once. Order totals include Registered, In Progress and Completed only; drafts and cancelled excluded; accepted-quote orders and direct orders counted once each. Profit and cost metrics use only orders with complete captured estimates, weighted margin = total estimated profit / total net selling value; coverage displayed. Quote totals shown separately, provisional totals marked. Activity uses stored customer, enquiry, quotation review/dispatch/price changes/revision archives, order and supplier offer histories and settings versions; no fabricated actions or actors; unscoped customer/settings events visible only without scope filters; identical inherited events deduplicated. No comprehensive global audit yet. Advanced rules and approval policies remain Phase 3.
